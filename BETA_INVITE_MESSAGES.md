# Ride Radar — Beta Tester Invite Messages

---

## 1. Discord Message

🛵 **Ride Radar beta is live.**

https://www.rideradarapp.com

**First, install it:** Open the link, then "Add to Home Screen" (iOS Share menu / Android Chrome menu). It works way better as an app than in a browser tab.

**Test these in order:**
1. Sign up → complete your profile
2. Check the Radar map — your location should show
3. Create one signal (meetup, warning, or bike down)
4. Send a message to another tester
5. Install as PWA if you haven't already

**Found a bug?** Settings → Report an Issue / Feedback sends a pre-filled email with your device info. Or drop it in this channel.

**Report immediately if:** login fails, blank screen on open, map won't load, messages won't send, or the app crashes.

**Safety note:** Ride Radar is a community awareness tool, not an emergency service. Always use your own judgment on the road. In an emergency, call 911.

Thanks for riding with us. Every report makes the app better.

## 2. Email Invite

Subject: You're in — Ride Radar beta

Hey rider,

Ride Radar is ready for real-world testing, and you're on the list.

**Get started:** https://www.rideradarapp.com

**For the best experience, install it as an app:**
- **iPhone (Safari):** Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the menu → "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

**What to test first:**
1. Sign up and complete your rider profile
2. Open the Radar — check that the map loads and your location appears
3. Create a signal (a meetup, road warning, or bike-down alert)
4. Message another beta tester
5. Check that notifications work when someone messages you

**How to report bugs:**
Go to Settings → "Report an Issue / Feedback" inside the app. It auto-attaches your device info, which saves us a lot of back-and-forth. One bug per report, please.

**Urgent issues — tell us right away:**
- Cannot log in or sign up
- Blank white screen on open
- Map does not load
- Creating a signal fails completely
- Messages fail to send
- App crashes or closes unexpectedly

**Safety reminder:**
Ride Radar is built by riders, for riders. It is a community awareness tool — not an emergency dispatch service, not a replacement for your own judgment, and not guaranteed to be accurate or up-to-date. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

We typically respond to bug reports within 24–48 hours.

Ride safe,
The Ride Radar team
