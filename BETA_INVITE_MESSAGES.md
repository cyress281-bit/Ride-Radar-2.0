# Ride Radar — Beta Tester Invite Messages

---

## 1. Discord / Community Post

🛵 **Ride Radar beta is open.**

https://www.rideradarapp.com

**Install it first.** Open the link, then add to your home screen — iOS via Share → "Add to Home Screen," Android via Chrome menu → "Install app." It runs better as an app than in a browser tab.

**What to test:**
- **Radar** — map loads, your location shows, broadcasts appear
- **Create a meetup or event** — title, time, address autocomplete, pin on map
- **Messaging** — start a chat from a rider's profile, send a photo
- **Notifications** — allow them, then have someone message you while the app is backgrounded
- **PWA install and update flow** — install from browser, check that updates pick up on relaunch

**How to report bugs:** Settings → "Report an Issue / Feedback" sends a pre-filled email with your device info. One bug per report. Screenshots help.

**Tell us immediately if:** the app crashes, login fails, the map stays blank, or messages won't send.

**Safety note:** Ride Radar is a community awareness tool. It is not an emergency service. Always use your own judgment on the road. In an emergency, call 911.

Thanks for testing. Every report makes the ride better.

## 2. Email Invite

Subject: You're in — Ride Radar beta

Hey rider,

You're on the list for the Ride Radar beta. Here's everything you need.

**Get the app:** https://www.rideradarapp.com

**Install it as an app for the best experience:**
- **iPhone (Safari):** Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the menu → "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

**What to test:**

| Feature | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| **Radar** | Map loads, your location appears, broadcasts show on the map and in the list |
| **Meetup / event creation** | Create an event with a title, time, and address — the autocomplete should suggest addresses as you type. Verify the pin appears on the map. |
| **Messaging** | Open a rider's profile and tap Message. Send text and a photo. Check that the conversation list updates. |
| **Notifications** | Allow notifications when prompted. Have another tester message you while the app is in the background. You should get a banner. |
| **PWA install and updates** | Install from the browser, launch from the home screen, and check that app updates apply when you relaunch the app. |

**How to report bugs:**
Inside the app, go to Settings → "Report an Issue / Feedback." It auto-fills your app version, device, and screen size — just describe what happened and how to reproduce it. One bug per report, please. Screenshots and screen recordings are always welcome.

**Urgent issues — contact us immediately:**
- App crashes to home screen or closes unexpectedly
- Cannot log in or sign up
- Map stays blank or tiles never load
- Messages fail to send or never arrive
- Cannot install the app to your home screen

**Safety disclaimer:**
Ride Radar is built by riders, for riders. It is a community awareness tool — not an emergency dispatch service, not a replacement for your own judgment, and not guaranteed to be accurate or up-to-date. Signal accuracy depends on user reports. In an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

We typically respond to bug reports within 24–48 hours.

Ride safe,
The Ride Radar team
