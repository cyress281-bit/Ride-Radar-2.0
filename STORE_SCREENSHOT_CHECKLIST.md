# Store Screenshot Checklist

Use test data only. Keep the app clean, dark, and readable.

Suggested capture order:
1. Radar map with nearby ride signals
2. Create signal menu
3. Plan Meetup/Event with map pin
4. Bike Down detail with safety reminder
5. Messages
6. Profile
7. Official event badge / review request
8. Settings with account deletion and support

Capture rules:
- Do not show real private messages, emails, phone numbers, home addresses, or accident details.
- Do not show admin pages in public marketing screenshots.
- Do not imply emergency dispatch or guaranteed safety.
- Use the dark/neon Ride Radar visual style consistently.
- Keep overlays short and legible on phone screens.

Recommended test content:
- City Rider
- Night Shift Rider
- Weekend Rider
- Saturday Bike Night
- Out for a short ride
- Looking for wrench help
- Gravel on exit ramp
- Stopped on shoulder, need tow
