## 2. Full App Description

### Apple App Store (4,000 characters max)

Ride Radar is a real-time awareness and community network built for motorcyclists. See what is happening on the road around you, connect with nearby riders, and never ride alone unless you want to.

**LIVE RADAR**
The map shows real-time signals from the riding community — road hazards, traffic conditions, police presence, and rider meetups. Everything is time-stamped and expires automatically so you are never looking at stale information.

**CREATE SIGNALS**
Drop a pin for other riders to see. Post a meetup, warn about debris in the road, or send an urgent bike-down alert. Add photos, set locations, and choose how long your signal stays active.

**RIDER MESSAGING**
Message other riders directly. Start a conversation from their profile or respond to a meetup. No phone numbers exchanged — everything stays inside the app.

**MEETUPS & EVENTS**
Find group rides, bike nights, and events near you. Create your own and see who is coming. Get notified when someone RSVPs.

**LIVE LOCATION SHARING**
Optionally share your real-time location with friends so they can see where you are on the map. Both riders must opt in — privacy is always in your control.

**PWA SUPPORT**
Ride Radar works as a Progressive Web App. Install it directly from Safari or Chrome — no app store required. It feels like a native app and works offline for recently viewed areas.

Ride Radar is built by riders, for riders. It is a community awareness tool, not a replacement for safe riding judgment or emergency services.

Ride Radar installs as a Progressive Web App (PWA) directly from your browser. Tap "Add to Home Screen" in Chrome or "Share → Add to Home Screen" in Safari. The app works in standalone mode with push notifications, offline map caching, and native-feeling navigation.

## 4. Safety / Legal Disclaimer Copy

### Short Version (for store listing)

> Ride Radar is a community awareness tool, not an emergency service. Always ride within your limits, follow local traffic laws, and contact emergency services in case of accident or injury. Signal accuracy depends on user reports and may not reflect current conditions.

### Full Version (for in-app settings / legal page)

> **Ride Radar Safety Disclaimer**
>
> Ride Radar is designed to increase rider awareness through community-generated signals. It is not a substitute for safe riding practices, professional training, or emergency services.
>
> **Important:**
> - Signal accuracy and timeliness depend on user submissions. A signal may be outdated, incorrect, or missing.
> - Road conditions change rapidly. Always verify hazards visually before reacting.
> - Ride Radar does not provide turn-by-turn navigation or route planning.
> - In case of emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.
> - The app uses your location to show nearby signals and enable optional presence sharing. Location data is never sold or shared with third parties.
>
> By using Ride Radar, you acknowledge that you are solely responsible for your riding decisions and safety.

## 8. App Icon Guidance

### Requirements

| Platform | Size | Format | Background |
|----------|------|--------|------------|
| iOS App Store | 1024×1024 | PNG | Transparent or solid #050508 |
| Google Play | 512×512 | PNG | Transparent or solid #050508 |
| PWA | 192×192, 512×512, 1024×1024 | PNG | Solid #050508 (maskable for Android) |
| Adaptive Android | Foreground + background layers | SVG/PNG | Background #050508, foreground #39FF14 |

### Design Direction

- **Shape:** Rounded square (iOS) / adaptive circle/squircle (Android)
- **Colors:** Neon green (#39FF14) signal icon on near-black (#050508) background
- **Motif:** Simplified radar pulse or motorcycle silhouette — instantly readable at 48×48
- **No text** in the icon itself
- **High contrast** for accessibility and visibility on dark home screens
